Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026.

tiger-drive is used only by its owner to back up the owner's own files to the owner's own Google Drive.

  • Data accessed: only files that tiger-drive itself created in Google Drive (drive.file scope). No other files, contacts, email or profile data are read.
  • Use: uploading the owner's backup files and downloading them back to the owner's computer.
  • Sharing: data is not shared with, sold to, or shown to any third party.
  • Storage: files stay in the owner's Google Drive; the access token is kept only on the owner's server and computer.
  • Revoking access: at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
